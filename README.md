# CarryHub

> Smart Logistics Platform for the Textile Industry

## Vision

CarryHub is a B2B logistics platform built specifically for the textile industry.

It connects textile manufacturers, wholesalers and courier partners on a single platform to simplify same-day deliveries, reduce logistics costs and provide complete operational visibility.

Instead of managing deliveries through phone calls and WhatsApp groups, businesses can create delivery requests, assign couriers, track operations and analyze logistics performance from one dashboard.

---

## Problem

Textile companies currently face several operational challenges:

- Manual courier coordination
- High and inconsistent courier prices
- Delivery delays
- Lack of shipment tracking
- Communication through phone calls and WhatsApp
- No reporting or operational visibility

---

## Solution

CarryHub digitizes the entire logistics workflow.

Companies can:

- Create delivery requests
- Track courier status
- Monitor deliveries
- Analyze logistics performance
- Reduce operational costs

Couriers can:

- Accept nearby jobs
- Manage deliveries
- Build performance history
- Receive secure payments
